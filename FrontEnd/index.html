How to use BitIO

Browse products - scroll down past the 3D boards to see the full catalog. Use the filter buttons or search bar to find what you need.

Product details - click anywhere on a product card to open its detail view, where you can set a quantity before adding to cart.

3D boards - click any board in the hero section to inspect it up close. Drag to rotate. Click the hotspot dots to see what each component is.

Cart - click the Cart link in the nav. You can change quantities, remove items, or clear the whole cart before checking out.

Account - sign up or sign in via the nav link. Your addresses and notification preferences are saved under Settings.

Admin - if your account has admin access, you'll see the full dashboard at /admin.html with product management, orders, and returns.
